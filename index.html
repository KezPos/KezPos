    Why KezPos

    The soul of the app

    Cashier Mode

    Connect multiple cashier devices to your admin account over your local network. No internet needed, no shared logins. Cashiers see only what they need to work the till, sales and new customers. No access to inventory, pricing, or reports. Full admin control stays on the admin device.

    Customer Credit

    Save a customer's name and number once. Every debt sale is logged to their profile. Find them by name or number at checkout. No notebook, no guessing who owes what.

    Offline-First, Auto-Synced

    No internet? Keep selling. KezPos stores everything locally and syncs automatically every 10 minutes when you're back online. Your data is never stuck on one device.

    Supports Electronics Resellers

    Capture IMEI or serial number, condition, and warranty length per device sold. The warranty terms and a signature space print straight onto the receipt. Proof of sale and proof of warranty, in one slip.

    High-Value Sales

    Selling phones or electronics?
    We've got the paperwork.

    Every unit gets its own record. Serial number, condition, and warranty. And it all prints straight onto the customer's receipt. No more handwritten warranty slips.

    • IMEI / Serial Number per Item
      Log the exact unit sold. Critical for phones, laptops, and any device with a unique identifier.
    • Condition Tracking
      Mark each item new, used, or refurbished. So pricing and expectations are clear from the sale.
    • Warranty Length on the Receipt
      Set a warranty period per sale. 7 days, 3 months, 1 year. It prints automatically on the customer's copy.
    • Signature Space Built In
      A signature line appears on the receipt for both parties to sign off. Useful proof if a warranty claim comes up later.
